# BitTV — debloat list for the TCL 55C6K-UK (Google TV 14, "Smart TV Pro").
# Adapted from cleanyourtv's TCL 55C7K-UK list (same G08 platform, firmware UTT2.x):
#   https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/blob/main/examples/tcl-55c7k-uk/debloat.txt
#
# Every line is DISABLED with `pm disable-user --user 0`, never uninstalled. restore.sh re-enables all of them.
# Packages that are not on the TV are skipped. Comment a line out (#) to keep that app.
# Lines marked [ask] are Ludovico's call — they are commented out until he decides.
#
# KEEP (never in this list): com.google.android.katniss (Google Assistant / mic button — he wants it),
# com.google.android.apps.mediashell (Chromecast), com.tcl.tv + com.tcl.tvinput (HDMI / Live TV),
# all com.mediatek.*, com.google.android.tv.remote.service (the remote's mic), Netflix, Prime Video,
# the UK catch-up apps, com.android.vending (Play Store).

# --- TCL tracking and analytics -------------------------------------------------------------

com.tcl.logkit                         # log capture and upload
